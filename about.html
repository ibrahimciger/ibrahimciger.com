Istanbul University · Master's Thesis · 2015
An Investigation on the Economical Life
of the Holy Prophet
Halil İbrahim Ciğer  ·  MA Islamic Law & Finance  ·  Istanbul University 2015
Preface

This thesis examines the economic dimensions of the Prophet Muhammad's life, drawing on classical Islamic sources alongside contemporary economic analysis.

Chapter I · Pre-Prophetic Period

Examines trade practices in 6th-century Arabia, the Prophet's early commercial activities, partnership arrangements, and the ethical foundations he established before his prophethood.

Chapter II · Medina Period

Analyses the construction of the Medina market, prohibition of monopolistic practices, introduction of the mudd and sa' standards, and foundational principles of Islamic economic justice.

Chapter III · Fiscal Policy

Covers zakat administration, spoils distribution, land tax, and the Prophet's approach to public finance and wealth redistribution.

Chapter IV · Legacy

Discusses the enduring influence of prophetic economic principles on Islamic finance, waqf institutions, and contemporary Muslim-majority economies.

Conclusion

The Prophet's economic model demonstrates a coherent system balancing spiritual imperatives with pragmatic governance.

Master's Thesis · Istanbul University 2015
